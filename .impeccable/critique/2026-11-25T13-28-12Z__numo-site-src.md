## Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict: Polished, category-interchangeable fintech — clean craft on the surface, but detached from Numo's product truth and in direct contradiction with `DESIGN.md`.**

**LLM assessment (unanchored):** The `redesign` branch didn't iterate on `DESIGN.md` ("The Signwriter's Counter" — Bebas Neue + Sora, Awning Cream/Butcher Paper ground alternation, 2rem/3rem noise-textured slabs, pill buttons, Fresh Paint Mint *One Coat Rule*, running illustrations); it quietly replaced it with a restrained, Sora-only Square/Linear editorial system (`--paper` `#FBFAF7`, `10px` hairline buttons, `14px` bordered white cards, frosted-glass nav, 3D Blender renders) while leaving `DESIGN.md` untouched (and even citing `DESIGN.md` in `Showcase.tsx:11` for a Blender kit `DESIGN.md` never mentions). The redesign undeniably improved grown-up readability over loud all-caps Bebas Neue, moving closer to `PRODUCT.md`'s *"Oh — this is real"* goal on the Hero and Footer. Fresh Paint Mint (`#5EFFC2`) is functionally extinct across ~6,000px of unbroken `#FBFAF7` paper, and the five Showcase rows stitch together three incompatible visual worlds (3D Blender commercial terminals, raw 2D kinetic poster type, and tiny flat iOS notifications).

## Overall Impression

Claude's redesign gave Numo a calm, credible typographic foundation — the Hero, Footer, and `/setup` prose genuinely feel grown-up and free of crypto-hype clichés. Yet the UI currently suffers from an identity and truth crisis: it looks like an ultra-safe Square/Stripe wrapper with virtually all of Numo's signature mint warmth stripped out, its `/btcpay` centerpiece clips off its own punchline while trapping keyboard focus, and its visuals repeatedly contradict its copy (dedicated SUNMI hardware next to *"on the phone you already have"*, giant unslashed *"2.9% 30¢"* beside *"Zero platform fees"*, and undocumented Stripe cards). The single biggest opportunity is to **re-anchor the evolved Sora design system in Numo's real counter-level character** (deliberate warm ground cadence + disciplined Fresh Paint Mint coat + unified everyday-Android visuals) while repairing the functional breaks on `/btcpay`, `/setup`, and the download funnel.
